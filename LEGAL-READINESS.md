# Fair Launch legal-review brief

This document is a factual brief for qualified counsel, not legal advice.

## Product facts counsel must evaluate

- A public, multi-creator platform intended to let third parties configure and launch Solana Token-2022 assets.
- A fixed platform fee of 0.1 SOL per successful policy initialization, paid to the disclosed JamdDmaj treasury.
- Mandatory creator and eligible early-participant vesting, liquidity locks, anti-Sybil eligibility commitments and public verification.
- Optional visibility/analytics services; no promise of price, profit, liquidity or exchange listing.
- Wallets are self-custodial. JamdDmaj must not request or store seed phrases or private keys.
- Creators can provide names, symbols, images, websites and social links, which creates moderation and intellectual-property obligations.

## Questions requiring written advice

1. Which entity operates the platform and in which jurisdictions may it serve creators and buyers?
2. When could a created token or platform activity be regulated as a security, commodity, payment instrument, money transmission, crowdfunding or another financial service?
3. Which creator verification, sanctions screening, KYC/AML, geoblocking and record-retention duties apply?
4. Which consumer disclosures, fee disclosures, refund rules, tax records and complaint procedures are required?
5. What terms of service, privacy policy, acceptable-use policy, risk disclosure and creator representations are required?
6. What moderation, takedown, trademark/copyright and prohibited-project process is required?
7. May the planned anti-Sybil provider process identity data, and what consent, minimization, retention and breach duties apply?
8. Does the JamdDmaj/JDMAJ token require a separate opinion covering utility, distribution, governance and marketing?

## Evidence to retain

- Counsel identity, engagement date, covered jurisdictions and written opinion reference.
- Approved product flow and exact release/commit reviewed.
- Required policies, restricted territories and operational controls.
- Re-review triggers for contract, fee, custody, token-economics or jurisdiction changes.

Do not place privileged legal advice or personal identity documents in the public repository. Record only a non-sensitive reference in `security/mainnet-readiness.json` after counsel approves the release.
