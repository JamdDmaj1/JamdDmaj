# JamdDmaj AI v1.19.0

Trades-only update focused on the Pro Signals panel.

- Added many more crypto assets for manual trade search.
- Added manual stock/ETF search support, including major tech, finance, energy, consumer, and index symbols.
- Improved trade scoring from 6 to 8 technical checks: trend, EMA alignment, price position, MACD, RSI, move, and volume.

Educational scanner only. It does not execute orders and is not financial advice.
