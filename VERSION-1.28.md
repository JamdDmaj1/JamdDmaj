# JamdDmaj AI v1.28.0

- The Pro server combines Binance and Bitget USDT perpetual catalogs and tolerates partial exchange failures.
- Volume confirmation is mandatory; low-market-cap setups require stricter liquidity and quality.
- Market cap, venue, and clear micro/small-cap warnings appear in the app and Telegram.
- Suggested invalidation is no longer treated as an automatically executed stop.
- Telegram no longer sends call-expired messages.
- At roughly +3% estimated ROE at 10x, Telegram suggests a protected-profit level and reports a protected close if price retraces.
- Confirmed 1h/4h reversals are reported and can unlock an opposite setup when every filter passes.
- Pro includes a separate news, sentiment, dominance, and macro-context panel.
- GitHub can build a native unsigned iPhone IPA for private sideloading.
