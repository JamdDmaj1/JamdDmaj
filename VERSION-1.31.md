# JamdDmaj AI 1.31.0

## General

- Settings are divided into clear tabs instead of one long page.
- Managed JamdDmaj access hides the unnecessary OpenRouter connection from regular users.
- Sync IDs and recovery codes stay masked on screen and can be copied when needed.
- Mobile header controls and the Markets panel use space more cleanly.

## Pro mode

- Signal monitoring now records confirmed invalidations, partial wins and ambiguous candles honestly.
- Verified win rate excludes unresolved and ambiguous calls.
- Telegram notifications are deduplicated across overlapping server runs.
- Tokenized commodities and equities receive the correct category.
- Unknown market-cap assets must pass stricter score and liquidity filters.
- Paper positions use configurable percentage risk and estimated invalidation distance.
- A nightly walk-forward backtest runs independently from the phone.

The Pro scanner remains educational and paper-only. It does not place real orders or guarantee outcomes.
