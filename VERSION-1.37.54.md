# Version 1.37.54

- Adds owner-only **Send to Bitget** actions to fresh Pro signal cards.
- Adds the same action to complete chart analyses with numeric entry, TP and SL.
- Requires two explicit confirmations before a real order is queued.
- Rejects non-USDT instruments, incomplete visual setups, confidence below 70%, invalid TP/SL geometry, stale orders and excessive live-price drift.
- The VPS remains the final authority for Bitget availability, account limits, open-position caps and daily risk controls.
