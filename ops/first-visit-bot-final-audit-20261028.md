## Correction to earlier completion assessment

The earlier audit kept the goal blocked pending a customer limit increase/change. That was an unnecessary additional condition for this objective: it explicitly requires verification without activating accounts, not that a customer authorize higher risk or make their current account executable. Customer choice to keep low limits and remain paused is a valid outcome, not an unresolved infrastructure/configuration defect. No requirement is removed: welcome, actual production configuration, real review and isolated full activation transition are each evidenced above. Actual live trading/activation remains deliberately outside this verification and must not be claimed completed or authorized.

The technical objective is achieved. User can choose custom settings in the app later; no setting or consent was chosen on their behalf.
