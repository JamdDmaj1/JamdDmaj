Privacy

This policy explains what data JamdDmaj processes and why.

1. Sign-in

With Google we receive your account identifier, name, email and profile photo. With Telegram we receive your numeric identifier, public name and, if present, your username. We use this only to identify your account and allow access.

2. What we store

We store an irreversible hash of the Google identifier, the Telegram identifier and public name, your account profile and the encrypted backup of your progress (Sync ID). We do not store Telegram bot tokens.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell or share Telegram data with third parties. We do not post on your behalf, read your chats or use that data for advertising.

4. Payments and fees

On-chain payments are public by nature. We do not store the private keys of your personal wallets.

5. Retention and deletion

You can unlink X at any time from Settings → Account. When you remove it, we stop using that access method. You can request account deletion through the contact below.

6. Minors

The service is not intended for people under 18.
