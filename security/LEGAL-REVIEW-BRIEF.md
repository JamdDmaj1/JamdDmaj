# Legal review brief — JAMD

Status: counsel intake document only; it is not legal advice or an opinion.

Intended operating location: Miami, Florida, United States. Proposed asset:
fixed-supply Solana Token-2022 token named Jamd, symbol JAMD, intended for
JamdDmaj product utility and AI-credit payments. The current implementation is
Devnet-only and valueless; no Mainnet mint, sale or liquidity is authorized.

Counsel must determine before launch:

- token characterization under applicable US federal and Florida law;
- whether any sale, distribution, rewards, staking, redemption or liquidity
  activity triggers securities, money-transmission, commodities, tax or
  consumer-protection obligations;
- permitted regions, age limits, sanctions screening, disclosures, marketing
  claims, refund policy and record retention;
- privacy roles and contracts for the external uniqueness provider;
- terms for AI-credit purchases with JAMD and treatment of price volatility;
- entity, beneficial ownership, treasury controls and incident obligations.

The opinion reference may be recorded privately, but the public readiness file
must identify counsel, covered jurisdictions, date and whether launch is
approved, restricted or rejected. Restrictions must be enforced before Mainnet.
